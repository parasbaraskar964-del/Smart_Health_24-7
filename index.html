About Our SmartHealth Platform

SmartHealth is a comprehensive digital healthcare ecosystem designed to streamline patient consultations, diagnostic triage, and prescription management into a single, unified platform. By integrating instant AI symptom analysis with real-time video consultations and automated e-prescriptions, SmartHealth removes physical barriers to high-quality healthcare.

Platform Highlights & Features

🤖 Instant AI Triage

Ask health questions and describe symptoms anytime. Our AI provides instant preliminary guidance and health recommendations.

📹 HD Telemedicine

📝 e-Prescription Sheet
